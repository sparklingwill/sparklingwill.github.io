Support

Help with Photo Gotcha, the mobile app by Sparklingwill LLC. If something is not working, or you want a generated photo reviewed, start here.

Contact us

Email help@sparklingwill.com and we will get back to you. To help us find the problem quickly, please include:

  • Your app version, shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
  • The email address your account uses.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Report a generated photo

If the App generates something that looks wrong or inappropriate, you can report it from inside the app: tap the photo to open it full-screen, then tap Report and choose a reason. You can add a note if you want to explain.

We keep the report so we can review it and adjust what the App generates. Reports are covered in our Data retention section.

Delete your account

You can delete your account instantly in the app: open Settings and tap Delete Account. If you no longer have access to the app, or you only want certain data removed, use our Delete Account & Data page.

Privacy and terms

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how long we keep it. Our Terms of Use cover credits, acceptable use, and your rights.
