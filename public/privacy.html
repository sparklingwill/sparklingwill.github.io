Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sparklingwill LLC ("Sparklingwill," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Photo Gotcha mobile app (the "App").

1. Who we are

Photo Gotcha is operated by Sparklingwill LLC. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at help@sparklingwill.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, and your display name and profile picture if available. If you register with a password, we store a securely hashed version of it (never the password itself).
  • Photos you upload. To generate an image, you upload one or two reference photos. These are processed to create your result (see Section 4) and are automatically deleted shortly afterward (see Section 6).
  • Generated images. The images the App creates from your photos, stored temporarily so you can view and save them.
  • Purchase information. When you buy credits, the transaction is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We receive a record of the purchase (product, transaction identifier, and status) so we can grant your credits. We never receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Usage and technical data. Basic records needed to run the service, such as the styles you select and timestamps of generation requests, used to operate credits and troubleshoot problems.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate the images you request.
  • To create and manage your account and credit balance.
  • To process purchases and grant the credits you buy.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.

4. How your photos are processed and shared

To create your result, the photos you upload are sent to BytePlus (an AI image service operated by ByteDance), which generates the image and returns it to us. Because this is a cloud-based service, your photos may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence.

We also rely on the following service providers:

  • Cloudflare R2 — secure, private storage for uploaded and generated images. Images are stored in a private bucket and are not publicly accessible.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat — to process in-app purchases and validate transactions.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google.

We share your information with these providers only as needed to deliver the App's features. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

5. Data retention

  • Uploaded photos and generated images are automatically deleted within 7 days. The temporary links used to process them expire much sooner.
  • Account information is kept for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your account data and associated records are removed.
  • Purchase records may be retained as required for accounting, tax, and legal compliance.

6. Your rights and choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, including deletion of your account. To make a request, email us at help@sparklingwill.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including private storage, encrypted connections, and short-lived access links to images. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorized access.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may be communicated within the App.
