Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This page lets you request deletion of your account and associated data for Photo Gotcha, the mobile app operated by Sparklingwill LLC. You can also request deletion of specific data without closing your account.

Fastest option: delete in the app

You can delete your account instantly from inside Photo Gotcha: open Settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, credit balance, and generated images straight away — no email needed. Use the form below if you no longer have access to the app, or if you only want specific data removed.

How to request deletion by email

  1. Fill in the form below with the email address tied to your Photo Gotcha account.
  2. Choose whether you want to delete your entire account or only specific data.
  3. Press Submit request. Your email app opens with a pre-filled message addressed to help@sparklingwill.com — send it to complete your request.
  4. We confirm your identity (that you control the account email) and process your request within 30 days, then email you to confirm completion.

Prefer to email us directly? Send your request to help@sparklingwill.com and include your account email and what you would like deleted.

What is deleted, and what we keep

When you ask us to delete your account, we delete:

  • Account information — your email address, and the display name and profile picture received from Google Sign-In if you used it; and your securely hashed password if you registered with one. We keep a one-way hash of your email address only, as described below.
  • Credit balance and your generation history and usage records (the styles you selected and request timestamps).
  • Uploaded photos and generated images. These are already deleted automatically within 7 days of creation, so in most cases none remain by the time we process your request.

If you request partial deletion, we delete only the data you specify and leave your account active.

Some data may be kept after deletion where the law requires it or for legitimate operational reasons:

  • Purchase records. We keep the record of each purchase — the store, the transaction and event identifiers, the event and purchase types, the product, credits granted, environment, status and date. Your account id and email address are removed from these records, including from the stored payment-provider record, and we note the date of that removal; the anonymized record is deleted approximately 2 years later. Payment card details are never collected or stored by us.
  • Safety-flagged content. Our AI processing provider (BytePlus) may retain content flagged by automated safety filters for a limited period (up to 180 days) as required for safety and legal compliance. This is outside our control.
  • Backups and logs. Residual copies may persist in routine backups for a short period before being overwritten on our normal cycle.
  • Content reports. If you reported a generated photo, the report — the reason, any note you wrote, the style used, and a reference to the photo — is kept so we can review it, but your account id and email address are removed from it when your account is deleted.
  • Server request logs. Our per-request server log, which includes your IP address, is kept for approximately 180 days. Your account id and email address are removed from these records when your account is deleted, and what remains no longer identifies you.
  • A one-way hash of your email address. This cannot be reversed to recover your address. We keep it for one purpose only: so that one-time rewards (signup credits and the referral bonus) cannot be claimed again by deleting and recreating an account.

For full details on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
